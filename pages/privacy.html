Privacy Policy &
Terms of Use

This policy outlines how data is handled on this site, GDPR compliance details, and the intellectual property protection governing the contents.

Self-Hosted Security

All data is stored locally on a secure private Synology NAS in Switzerland, with no third-party cloud hosting.

No Third-Party Trackers

No third-party analytics or advertising cookies. First-party, anonymous usage data (page views, approximate country, device/browser type, on-page engagement) is stored locally to improve the site.

Strict Data Privacy

Contact information is used solely to verify identity and communicate. It is never sold or shared.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Minimal personal data is collected: name, email, and message (contact form), used solely to enable professional communication. The site also records anonymous, first-party usage analytics — approximate country (derived from network routing, not your IP address), device/browser/OS type, referring site, and on-page engagement such as time on page and scroll depth — to understand how the site is used. This data is never shared with third parties.

2. GDPR & Swiss FADP Compliance

Aligned with Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and GDPR. You have the right to access and request erasure of your data at any time.

3. Intellectual Property & Copy Protection

All content is the exclusive intellectual property of Vinamra Kumar. Scrape protection measures are applied. Reproduction is strictly prohibited.
